# Ventus Motion Ad Storyboard

## Direction

This is a 24-second, 16:9 product film built around one visual idea: browsing noise becomes a focused workspace. The reference film's useful language is bold scale contrast, hard black and electric-color fields, kinetic type, masked product imagery, and short pauses between dense motion beats. The Ventus version keeps that energy while using its own blue-violet identity, logo ribbon, native browser shell, workspaces, AI sidebar, and local-first story.

All keyframes are 1672 x 941 PNG files. Treat the blue-violet ribbon as the continuous object connecting every shot.

## Animator AI Prompt Set

### Shot 1

Animate a fast forward camera move through the layered Ventus browser fragments in `01-chaos.png`. Use strong parallax, shallow depth of field, and directional blur. Tabs should converge on the glowing V at center-right. Add the exact headline `THE WEB GOT LOUD.` in oversized white geometric sans-serif type, appearing one word at a time. Keep the Ventus color palette and do not invent new interface elements.

### Shot 2

Morph the converging interface fragments into one continuous cobalt, cyan, and violet light ribbon. The ribbon forms the exact Ventus mark shown in `02-brand-formation.png`. Use smooth spline motion with a short overshoot and a calm hold. Add `MEET VENTUS.` on the left. Preserve the logo silhouette precisely.

### Shot 3

Transition through the center of the Ventus V into `03-product-reveal.png`. Reveal the authentic frameless dark browser window layer by layer: window outline, omnibox, vertical workspaces, new-tab content, then AI sidebar. Keep the camera almost straight-on so the interface stays readable. Add `ONE FOCUSED BROWSER.` above-left.

### Shot 4

Animate `04-workspaces-v2.png` as an organization sequence. Magnify the left workspace rail, then fan the workspace and tab cards from back to front. Snap each row into alignment with subtle elastic easing. Use only the visible Ventus interface. Add `BROWSE. ORGANIZE.` and avoid adding other product logos.

### Shot 5

Animate `05-ventus-ai.png` with a controlled left-to-right information flow. Highlight one passage on the page, trace the luminous line into the Ventus AI panel, and reveal the summary bullets in three staggered groups. Add `THINK WITH THE PAGE.` above-left. Do not use humanoid AI imagery or floating chat bubbles.

### Shot 6

Animate `06-local-first.png` as a secure local orbit. Tracking particles from the left should curve away from the protected boundary. The database stack, credential key, and download tile should rise gently in separate depth layers. Add `YOUR DATA. YOUR DEVICE.` on the left. Keep the mood confident rather than threatening.

### Shot 7

Resolve the circular orbit into `07-end-card.png`. Let the exact Ventus logo and wordmark remain perfectly stable. Add a slow light sweep around the ring and hold the tagline `BROWSE. ORGANIZE. THINK.` for at least two seconds. Do not add a URL or extra call to action unless the campaign later supplies one.

## Continuity Rules

- Use 24 or 30 fps and keep the full cut between 22 and 26 seconds.
- Maintain a left-to-right energy flow until Shot 6, where the motion closes into a circle.
- Use one ribbon asset across all shots so the sequence feels continuous.
- Prefer hard cuts hidden by wipes, zoom-throughs, masks, and shape morphs over cross-dissolves.
- Keep headlines as editable typography layers. Do not bake AI-generated text over the interface.
- Use `03-product-reveal.png` and `05-ventus-ai.png` as UI-fidelity anchors. Do not replace them with a generic dashboard.
- Preserve the exact Ventus logo and its purple-blue-cyan gradient in every shot.
- Keep safe margins of at least 8 percent for 16:9 delivery and social crops.

## Generation Notes

The images were generated with the built-in image generation workflow using the existing Ventus logo, campaign background, product-experience image, and capability image as visual references. The generation prompts specified premium 16:9 motion-design keyframes, the Ventus near-black/blue/violet/cyan palette, strong depth separation for animation, authentic UI architecture, and no unrelated browser branding or watermarks.
