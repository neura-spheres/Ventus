# Code Of Conduct

Ventus should be a useful and respectful open-source project. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and community spaces is expected to follow this code.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent, but ask for clarity when needed.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Help make issues and pull requests easier to understand.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Sexualized comments or unwanted attention
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeated disruption of project discussions
- Bad-faith reports, spam, or intentionally misleading claims

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, block users, or take other reasonable action when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Reports can be sent privately to the maintainers through the security or contact channel listed in `SECURITY.md`.
